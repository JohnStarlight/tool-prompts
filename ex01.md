# Structured JSON Output

## Prompt: List three cities with their populations.

### Answer:
Sure! Here are three cities and their approximate populations:

1. Tokyo – about 13.96 million people (city proper)
2. New York City – approximately 8.3 million people
3. Paris – around 2.1 million people

---

## Better Prompt: Return exactly three cities with their populations in JSON format. Use this structure and nothing else: [{"city": string, "population": number}]

### Answer:
```json
[
  {"city": "Tokyo", "population": 13960000},
  {"city": "New York City", "population": 8336817},
  {"city": "Paris", "population": 2102650}
]
```

---

## Observation:
The unstructured prompt returns a mix of prose, formatting, and approximate language ("about", "approximately") that cannot be reliably parsed. The structured prompt with an explicit JSON schema enforces a consistent, machine-readable format across all inputs — same keys, same types, no extra text.
